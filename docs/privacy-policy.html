Privacy Policy — Colonist Game Analyst

Last updated: April 9, 2026

Colonist Game Analyst (“the extension”) is a read-only browser extension for colonist.io and hexs.io. This policy describes how it handles information.

Summary

The extension is designed to process match-related data locally in your browser. It does not send your game content to servers operated by the extension author for analytics or profiling.

What the extension accesses

  • Game pages on permitted hosts (colonist.io, hexs.io, including subdomains) so it can observe WebSocket traffic the page already uses and read the on-page activity feed for analysis.
  • Chrome storage (session) to keep merged state so the side panel can display banks, hands, dice, and related summaries. Session storage is scoped to the browser session.
  • Tab information as needed to attach scripts only to eligible game URLs.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell personal data.
  • We do not operate a backend that receives your full game payloads for advertising.
  • The extension does not automate gameplay or submit moves on your behalf.

Export

If you use Export JSON, files are saved to your device through your browser’s download flow. You control where those files go.

Third parties

The extension interacts with Colonist / Hexs websites you already opened. Their privacy policies govern those services. This extension is not affiliated with Colonist.

Children

The extension is not directed specifically at children. Parental guidance applies as for any browser add-on.

Changes

We may update this policy when the extension’s behavior changes. The “Last updated” date will change accordingly.
